About Karlvest

Based in Accra, Karlvest specializes in preparing Ghanaian educators for successful international teaching careers. We understand the unique challenges teachers face when applying for positions abroad and provide comprehensive support throughout the entire process.

Our personalized approach ensures each teacher receives tailored guidance to meet global standards. From document preparation to cultural orientation, we equip you with everything needed to stand out in the competitive international education market.

Over 200 teachers successfully placed in international schools

Our 4-Step Preparation Process

We guide you through every stage of your international teaching journey

CV Review & Optimization

We transform your CV to meet international standards, highlighting your strengths in ways that appeal to global recruiters.

  • Professional achievement highlighting

Document Verification
